# Model Scope and Evidence

## Thermal Reference

The coupled model advances one resident SMA bed and one SE bed. Heat and fluid are routed by stage, while the solid temperatures, phase fractions, and finite fluid inventories remain in the state:

| Stage | SMA fluid path | SE fluid path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Source heat exchanger; forward through the SMA bed | Dwell |
| 2 | Source heat exchanger; forward through the SMA bed | Reverse through the SE bed, rejecting heat at the hot exchanger |
| 3 | Hot exchanger; reverse through the SMA bed | Dwell |
| 4 | Hot exchanger; reverse through the SMA bed | Forward through the SE bed and cold exchanger |

Fluid enthalpy is the integral of piecewise linear heat capacity; properties outside the specified table are rejected. Signed face fluxes telescope across each control volume. Coolant inventories, sensible and chemical solid energy, and source, ambient, and load heat are accounted for separately. The SMA and SE forces and displacements are matched through mechanical compatibility. The fixed cold reservoir logs the reaction heat used to maintain its temperature rather than resetting a warm state.

The reference prescribes transport and does not solve pump pressure, valve dynamics, or the full compressible equation of state. The independent material module checks energy identities at a prescribed phase fraction; it does not identify material kinetics or hysteresis.

## Native Connector Evidence

| Comparison | Conditions | Result |

This is not a closed-loop controller or pump-capacity test. |

The native comparisons are research evidence summarized here, not outputs generated by the Python example. Their archived inputs, Simulink operating points, and raw trajectories are not part of this distribution. The model source in `simscape/` is the conservative pipe component used in the finite-resistance fixture, not the complete fixture or device.

Hydraulic work is separate from heat delivered to the liquid; electrical input would additionally need a pump-efficiency model or measurement. A smaller cold-start mass swing does not establish periodic operation, higher cooling power, or hardware validation. A paired benchmark comparison requires a common modeled inventory and operating window, physically admissible periodic cycles, and independent zero-load and zero-lift checks.
